# Code Of Conduct

KaiCode contributors are expected to keep the project practical, respectful,
and welcoming.

## Standards

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, but correct problems clearly.
- Keep feedback about code and behavior, not personal traits.
- Avoid harassment, insults, threats, or sexualized language.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or
block participation when behavior undermines a safe and productive project.

Report conduct concerns to `me@kaicyrus.com`.
